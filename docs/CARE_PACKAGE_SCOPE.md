## Policies

- Unused change hours roll over max **one** month — never unlimited accrual
- Hours are not for full redesigns
- New features are extra unless included
- External provider costs separate
- No 24/7 emergency SLA unless separately contracted

All packages are **DRAFT** until commercially and legally approved.
